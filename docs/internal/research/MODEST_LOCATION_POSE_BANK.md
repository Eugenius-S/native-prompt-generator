## What Is Useful in the File

The main takeaway is not in individual lines, but in the system:
- modest prompts work well when the woman has a clear activity or physical context;
- the location is almost never abstract; it is "place + object scene + light";
- the pose is often not a "sexy pose" but an everyday or sporty action with a slight turn toward the camera.

## System for Modest Scenes

Scene formula:
- `place`
- `supporting objects`
- `light behavior`
- `camera anchor`

Working families:
- refined sport
- travel polished
- culture / bookish / music
- craft / hobby / maker
- cozy domestic
- elegant public interiors
- soft outdoor leisure

What makes a good modest scene:
- the heroine has something to do: reaching, holding, adjusting, writing, carrying, placing, fixing, walking;
- the environment reads quickly through 2-3 objects;
- the light is not random but specific: `crisp winter sunlight`, `soft shop daylight`, `warm backstage lighting`, `clean skylight`, `blue hour and warm lights`.

## System for Modest Poses

Modest pose formula:
- `task anchor`
- `body angle`
- `one active hand`
- `face return to camera`

Working patterns:
- she adjusts / ties / holds / pours / writes / carries / turns / reaches
- one knee bent / one step up / sideways seated / leaning against support
- direct eye contact is preserved, but the pose stays functional.

What works well:
- not "lying there and looking pretty", but doing something;
- the hands are busy with an object or support;
- the torso is slightly turned;
- the gaze returns to the camera.
